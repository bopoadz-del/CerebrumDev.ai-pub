## 3. Mechanism ties (existing law this slots into)

- **Certification states** (gold #2, Phase 4): `candidate → technically_verified → domain_review_required → domain_approved → deprecated`. Only `domain_approved` formulas and rules may be authoritative in production mode. **The paid tier is `domain_approved` packs.**
- **Billing entitlement** (Universal Kernel capability #21): the `require_entitled` gate enforces the paid line; uncertified packs carry honesty labels, never silent claims.
- **Delivery Standard**: the product's tier is declared in its platform block; the generator wires the gate at generation time.

## 4. What must never happen

- An uncertified (`candidate`) pack presented as certified — the honesty-label law applies to tiers.
- Paid gating on the **body** — the Universal Kernel is the floor under every product; the paid line is **domain certification**, not platform basics.
- Firing Phase 2 early — the Founder deferred it; the Universal Kernel run and the v0.1 control layer complete first.
